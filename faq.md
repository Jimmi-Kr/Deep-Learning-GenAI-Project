# Frequently Asked Questions

**Is this a group or individual project?**
Individual. Every student registers and submits separately.

**What accounts do I need before starting?**
Kaggle, GitHub, Weights & Biases, and Hugging Face (the last one only needed if you attempt the bonus deployment).

**Where do I train my model — Kaggle or Colab?**
Either. You can train separately on Kaggle or Colab, then upload the trained model to Kaggle Hub and import it into your inference notebook when submitting.

**What happens if I don't complete the setup (Kaggle + GitHub + W&B) and Form 1?**
You will not be eligible for evaluation or viva, regardless of your Kaggle score. See [Registration]({{ '/registration.html' | relative_url }}).

**How is my final score calculated?**
See the full breakdown on the [Grading]({{ '/grading.html' | relative_url }}) page — Kaggle Performance, Code Quality, Report, Viva, Milestones, and an optional Deployment bonus.

**Where can I find the most current, authoritative policy?**
Always check the [live evaluation policy document]({{ site.data.term.evaluation_policy_url }}) — it's updated independently of this site and takes precedence over anything here.

**I have a question not answered here — who do I ask?**
Reach out through the course's official communication channel (email/forum) rather than editing this page directly.
