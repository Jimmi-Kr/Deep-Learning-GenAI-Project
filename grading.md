# Grading Breakdown

| Component | Weightage | Evaluated By | Details |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kaggle Performance (KP) | 30 marks | Auto (Leaderboard) | Based on your relative best performance on the Kaggle leaderboard. Cutoff score for **{{ site.data.term.term_code }}**: **{{ site.data.term.kaggle_cutoff }}** |
| Notebook & Code Quality (C) | 5 marks | L1 Examiner | Quality of code in your GitHub repo/Notebook — readability, modularity, documentation, and reproducibility (`requirements.txt` / `environment.yaml`) |
| Report (R) | 15 marks | L2 Examiner | Concise technical report (max 5–6 pages): problem statement, methodology, model architecture, training details, evaluation, error analysis, insights. [Sample format]({{ site.data.term.report_sample_format_url }}) |
| Viva (V) | 40 marks | L2 Examiner | Individual understanding of model design, DL/GenAI concepts, justification of choices, and ability to extend/critique the solution |
| Milestones (M) | 10 marks | Auto & TA verify | Marks for meeting deadlines: registration, initial training/fine-tuning code, improved inference notebook, report, final submission |
| Bonus: Deployment | +5 marks | Auto & TA verify | Deploying the model/app (e.g., Hugging Face Space, Streamlit, Flask API) — evaluated on usability and stability |
