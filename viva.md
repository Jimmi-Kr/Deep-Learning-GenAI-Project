# Viva Process

Viva window for this term: **{{ site.data.term.viva_window }}**

Evaluation happens in two levels:

## L1 Examiner
Checks **Notebook & Code Quality** — readability, modularity, documentation, and reproducibility of your GitHub repo/notebook (`requirements.txt` / `environment.yaml`).

## L2 Examiner
Conducts the **Viva** (40 marks — the single largest component) and evaluates your **Report** (15 marks). Expect questions on:

- Your model design and architecture choices
- Core DL/GenAI concepts used in your solution
- Why you made the choices you did (over alternatives)
- Whether you can extend or critique your own solution on the spot

## Before your viva

- Make sure both vivas are completed within the deadline shown in the [Grading]({{ '/grading.html' | relative_url }}) page's deadline table.
- Re-read your own report and re-run your notebook end-to-end — examiners frequently ask you to justify specific lines of code or specific numbers in your report.
- Check the [current evaluation policy]({{ site.data.term.evaluation_policy_url }}) for any term-specific viva format changes.
